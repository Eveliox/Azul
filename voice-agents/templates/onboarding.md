## Owner: Request for call forwarding setup

Great! Here's what I need you to do on your phone:

**iPhone (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile):**
1. Open your Phone app
2. Dial: `*61*[VAPI_NUMBER]#` and tap Call
3. You'll hear a confirmation tone
4. That's it — missed calls now forward to the AI after 3-4 rings

**To turn it off later:** Dial `##61#`

The AI will only answer calls YOU don't pick up. If you answer, nothing changes.

Let me know when it's set up and I'll run a test call.
