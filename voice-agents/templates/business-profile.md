## Call handling rules

### What the AI should capture:
1. Caller's full name
2. Callback phone number (repeat back to confirm)
3. Service they're interested in
4. Preferred callback time
5. Urgency level (routine vs urgent)

### What the AI should NOT do:
- [ ] Quote prices or estimates
- [ ] Promise availability or appointments
- [ ] Discuss medical procedures in detail
- [ ] Make claims about results or outcomes
