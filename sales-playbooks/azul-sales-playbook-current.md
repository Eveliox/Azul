# Azul sales playbook

### Sell the confirmed business problem

Use this guide from first contact through signed scope and delivery handoff. The two services are Website Building and Speed-to-Lead Agent. Start by finding out how a homeowner reaches the business and how that enquiry becomes an estimate.

**English positioning.** "Azul builds bilingual websites and connects new website enquiries to a Speed-to-Lead Agent. It calls during your calling hours, asks about the job and books available estimates or passes the lead to your team."

**Spanish positioning.** "Azul crea sitios web bilingües y conecta las consultas del sitio con un agente que llama, pregunta por el trabajo y agenda estimados disponibles o pasa la consulta a su equipo, dentro del horario acordado."

### Choose the right path

| Buyer situation | Recommended conversation |
|---|---|
| Website needs replacing, enquiry handling works | Website scope and launch |
| Existing website works, form follow-up is slow | Agent connection and handoff |
| Both problems are confirmed | Combined website and agent proposal |
| Few enquiries and owner needs traffic | Clarify acquisition needs before recommending an agent |
| Buyer wants inbound reception or custom integrations | Verify feasibility before quoting |

**Internal prices.** Website $2,000 setup. Agent $3,500 setup plus $450/month. Combined package $4,500 setup plus $450/month. These reflect current offer settings reviewed November 11, 2026. Confirm usage, hosting, support and payment terms in writing. The public site hides prices.

### Working rules

Use factual observations. Demonstrate with your own phone and inbox. Identify demo results as demonstrations. Every active deal needs a problem, decision-maker and dated next step. Consult delivery before promising a custom workflow, text follow-up or launch date.

# Research and start a conversation

### Five-minute preparation

Confirm the business, trade, area, public business contact and likely decision-maker. Open the site on mobile. Check the contact button, service information, languages and enquiry path without submitting a fake customer enquiry. Write down one observation and one question.

### Manual call - English

"Hi, is this [Name]? I'm Evelio with Azul. I was looking at [Business] and noticed [verified observation]. May I ask one quick question about how website enquiries reach your team?"

If they agree, ask "When a homeowner sends that form while everyone is on a job, who calls them back?" Then "How is that working for you?"

If there is a relevant problem, say "We can walk through that process and show how a bilingual call and calendar handoff could work. Would [day/time] or [day/time] suit a 15-minute call?"

### Llamada manual - español

"Hola, ¿hablo con [Nombre]? Soy Evelio, de Azul. Estaba revisando [Negocio] y noté [observación verificada]. ¿Me permite una pregunta breve sobre cómo reciben las consultas de su sitio web?"

"Cuando alguien llena el formulario y el equipo está trabajando, ¿quién le devuelve la llamada? ¿Cómo les está funcionando ese proceso?"

"Podemos revisar el proceso y mostrarle cómo funcionaría una llamada bilingüe con la agenda de visitas. ¿Le conviene [día/hora] o [día/hora] para hablar 15 minutos?"

### If they are busy or someone else answers

Ask for the appropriate business time or the person responsible for website enquiries. Leave your name, Azul and callback number if invited to leave a message. Record the preferred channel and stop outreach when requested.

# Discover and qualify in 15 minutes

### Opening and agenda

EN - "I'd like to understand how enquiries become estimates, see where you want help, then show the relevant part of Azul. If it fits, we'll agree a next step. Does that work?"

ES - "Quiero entender cómo pasan de una consulta a un estimado, ver dónde necesitan ayuda y mostrarle la parte de Azul que corresponde. Si encaja, acordamos el próximo paso. ¿Le parece?"

### Questions to ask

1. Where do new enquiries come from? How many form leads arrived last month? Ask the owner to distinguish an estimate from a known count.
2. Who responds, and what happens when that person is busy? How long does it usually take? Ask for a recent example.
3. What languages do customers request? Which services and zip codes qualify?
4. How do you offer appointments? Which calendar is used, and who can share it?
5. What should always go to a human? Who can own those callbacks?
6. What is one additional completed job worth after direct job costs? What share of qualified estimates normally becomes work?
7. Who approves the purchase, what is the available budget and what date matters?

### Spanish questions to keep nearby

"¿De dónde llegan las consultas y cuántas recibieron el mes pasado?" "¿Quién llama y cuánto suele tardar?" "¿Qué pasa cuando esa persona está ocupada?" "¿Cómo confirman los horarios?" "¿Qué casos deben pasarle directamente?" "¿Quién más participa en la decisión?"

### Qualification gate

Advance when there is a confirmed problem, supported lead source, workable calendar or website scope, callback owner, buying authority and a plausible budget. Summarize the need using the buyer's wording. Record missing inputs before proposing.

EN - "You want [outcome] because [problem]. [Person] will handle exceptions, and you want this ready around [date]. Have I understood correctly?"

ES - "Quieren [resultado] porque [problema]. [Persona] atenderá los casos que necesiten ayuda y les gustaría estar listos para [fecha]. ¿Lo entendí bien?"

# Run a demo that matches the discovery

### Ten-minute agent demonstration

1. Repeat the buyer's problem and the source of the enquiry. Explain that the demonstration uses a controlled number.
2. Submit a test form with your listed number, approved permission and a test email. Show it appearing once in the correct workspace.
3. Answer the call. Show the business introduction, qualification and approved language path.
4. Choose an available test appointment. Open the calendar event and owner alert. Remove the test booking afterward.
5. Show a completed handoff example and the lead's next step. Explain who handles exceptions.
6. Show calling hours, retry settings and pause controls. For an Owner first demonstration, agree on the receiving test phone before ringing it.
7. Ask "Which part would change how your team works?" and "What would we need to test for you to feel comfortable?"

Prepare the demo in advance using the operating playbook. If an integration fails during the meeting, explain what failed, show a clearly labeled saved example if available and schedule a retest before acceptance.

### Website demonstration

Show the mobile path from service page to estimate request, the English and Spanish versions, and where the enquiry arrives. Use relevant portfolio work only when it is yours to show. Tie each feature to the owner's stated need. Confirm scope before discussing page counts, CMS or custom features.

### Boundaries to explain accurately

Calling follows the configured business time zone and hours. The core flow handles website or hosted-form leads. Booking depends on the connected calendar. The client's team owns exceptions. Verify texts and additional integrations in the specific deployment before including them.

### Economic conversation

Use contribution per completed job. Monthly break-even jobs equal incremental monthly cost divided by contribution per job. Include setup amortization when discussing the full commitment.

Example only - $450 monthly plus a $4,500 setup spread over 12 months is $825/month before other costs. At $600 contribution per extra completed job, that is 1.375 jobs, rounded to two whole jobs for monthly coverage. At a 40% estimate-to-job rate, the expected estimate requirement is 1.375 divided by 0.40, or about 3.44 additional estimates. The owner supplies the inputs. Track actual results after launch.

# Handle the decision and objections

### "It's expensive."

EN - "Is the concern the setup payment, monthly cost, or whether it will produce enough value? Let's compare the confirmed problem and your job economics with the proposed scope."

ES - "¿Le preocupa el pago inicial, la mensualidad o si le dará suficiente valor? Revisemos el problema, el margen de sus trabajos y el alcance."

### "I don't want AI speaking to customers."

EN - "Which part worries you most? We can test that situation. Owner first lets your phone ring first, with AI taking over if you miss the prompt. You can also pause calling."

ES - "¿Qué situación le preocupa más? Podemos probarla. Con la opción de llamar primero al dueño, su teléfono suena primero y la AI sigue si usted no responde al aviso. También puede pausar las llamadas."

### "We already have a receptionist or CRM."

EN - "What happens to a form lead when the team is busy? If that is already handled well, let's focus on any website need you mentioned."

ES - "¿Qué pasa con una consulta del formulario cuando el equipo está ocupado? Si ya lo resuelven bien, podemos concentrarnos en la necesidad del sitio que mencionó."

### "We need more leads."

EN - "Where do enquiries come from today? The website supports conversion and the agent follows up on enquiries received. Let's establish whether you have enough inbound demand for this to be useful."

ES - "¿De dónde llegan las consultas hoy? El sitio ayuda a convertir visitas y el agente da seguimiento a las consultas recibidas. Veamos si tienen suficiente demanda para que les sirva."

### "Send information" or "I need to think."

Ask what needs resolving and who else is involved. Send a short recap of their problem, relevant scope, price and next decision. Agree a review date if they are interested.

EN - "What would you need to review to make a decision? Shall we include [partner] and walk through it on [date]?"

ES - "¿Qué necesita revisar para decidir? ¿Incluimos a [socio] y lo vemos el [fecha]?"

# Propose, close and hand over

### Proposal contents

Write the business problem in the buyer's words. Specify the selected offer, scope, languages, form source, calendar, human handoff, implementation dependencies, acceptance checks, setup and recurring fees, payment milestones, usage treatment, support, cancellation and account ownership. Include buyer and Azul responsibilities with dates.

Use current agreed commercial terms. Any additional feature, discount, trial, refund or guarantee needs explicit written terms approved by the person responsible for delivery and pricing.

### Close the next decision

EN - "If the written scope covers [problem] at [price], are you ready to proceed? I'll send it by [date]. You'll review it with [person], and we can confirm the kickoff once the agreement and initial payment are complete."

ES - "Si el alcance escrito resuelve [problema] por [precio], ¿le gustaría avanzar? Se lo envío el [fecha]. Usted lo revisa con [persona] y confirmamos el inicio al completar el acuerdo y el pago inicial."

### Proposal follow-up

Within one business day, confirm receipt and the agreed review date. At the review, resolve the specific open question. If a buyer misses the agreed date, send a brief reminder and ask whether timing changed. After two unanswered follow-ups, close the active task and retain a dated nurture reason if appropriate.

### Delivery handoff

Pass along the signed scope, business and owner contacts, service selection, pricing and payment status, desired launch date, decision-maker, language, source of leads, calendar owner, exception owner, required access, promises made and acceptance criteria.

Use the client setup PDF, then the website and agent launch playbooks. Delivery confirms the launch date after reviewing inputs. Sales stays accountable for resolving any difference between what was sold and what can be delivered.

### First value review

After launch, review the first genuine enquiry with the owner. During the first month compare outcomes with the discovery problem. Ask for a testimonial or introduction after they can describe a result. Obtain approval for any public quote.

# Keep a pipeline you can act on

| Stage | Evidence to enter | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Researched | Relevant business and verified contact source | Make a personal first contact |
| Contacted | Dated outreach recorded | Follow the sequence or response |
| Discovery booked | Confirmed person and time | Prepare the relevant questions |
| Qualified | Problem, authority, fit, budget and timing understood | Demonstrate and scope |
| Proposal | Written scope and review date shared | Resolve the stated decision |
| Won | Signed agreement and agreed initial payment received | Complete delivery handoff |
| Lost or nurture | Decision or dated timing reason recorded | Close outreach or schedule revisit |

### Fields to record

Business, trade, location, language, website, contact source, decision-maker, current lead process, estimated or measured volume, problem, service fit, budget, offer value, objection, stage, last contact, next action, next action date, stop-contact preference and outcome.

### Daily routine

Review due follow-ups and delivery promises first. Research five new accounts. Make ten total personalized touches across new outreach and follow-up. Reserve meeting time for discoveries and demos. Update the record immediately after each conversation. These activity levels are starting targets for one founder.

### Weekly coaching

Review five conversation notes or authorized recordings. Check whether the rep asked about the process before pitching, confirmed a real problem, explained the demo accurately, reached the decision-maker and secured a dated next action.

Compare contacts, replies, meetings held, qualified proposals, paid starts and delivery hours. Choose one improvement for the following week. Reuse the prospect's exact phrasing where it clarifies the offer.

### Practice before using the scripts

Role-play an HVAC owner who is busy, a Spanish-speaking owner, a buyer with an existing receptionist and a skeptical owner concerned about AI. Practice the economic example with different inputs. Run the demo once from form submission to calendar and owner notification.

**Source basis and assumptions.** Internal offer settings and operating flows were reviewed November 11, 2026. The scripts, routines and targets are proposed sales practices. All bracketed fields require the prospect's actual details before use.
