## What the script must cover

### Phase 1: Pre-call prep (2 min BEFORE the call)
- What to have open on my screen when they join
- Which tabs / dashboards to pre-load
- What to review from their Loom audit notes

### Phase 2: Opening (0:00-1:00)
- How to greet them (warm, not corporate)
- Confirm their name, business, city
- Set the frame: "This is a 15-min call. I'll ask you a few things about your business, then show you what we'd fix. No pressure, no pitch — if it's a fit, I'll tell you the offer at the end. Sound good?"
- Bilingual opener: quickly ask "English or Español, whichever you prefer" — real proof of the moat

### Phase 3: Discovery (1:00-6:00) — the 5 questions I MUST ask
Draft the 5 highest-leverage discovery questions for a Miami home service owner. Frame them so I understand:
1. Their current lead sources (referrals? Google? paid ads?)
2. Their biggest pain (missed calls? no reviews? not showing up on Google?)
3. What they've tried and failed at (past agencies, ChatGPT, doing it themselves)
4. What their monthly marketing spend / budget looks like
5. What "success in 6 months" looks like for them

For each question, include:
- The exact question wording
- What I'm LISTENING for (the answer that means they're a fit)
- Red-flag answers that mean I should politely disqualify them

### Phase 4: Present the audit (6:00-10:00)
- What to show on screen-share (their Google Business Profile → their website → their reviews page)
- How to reference what I noticed in the Loom — reinforce that I did the homework
- Draw a line between their pain and the Azul solution: "You said missed calls are killing you — here's how our AI answering + text-back handles that"
- Show the Azul dashboard mockup on my site to visualize the outcome

### Phase 5: Present the offer (10:00-13:00)
- Introduce Growth Suite bundle
- Anchor: "à la carte this is $1,075/mo — bundled it's $549/mo — for Founding Clients like you, first 10 signups only, it's $399/mo locked for life"
- Explain what's included in plain owner-speak (not agency jargon)
- Mention: no contract, 30-day notice to cancel, free 7-day trial on AI Answering
- Payment: monthly Stripe subscription

### Phase 6: Handle objections (varies) — draft responses for these 6 common ones
1. **"I need to think about it"** — how to soft-close without being pushy
2. **"How is this different from [competitor / GHL / Podium]?"**
3. **"$399/mo is a lot, I've never spent that on marketing"**
4. **"I already have a website"** (Growth Suite is more than website)
5. **"Can you guarantee I'll get X leads?"** (never over-promise, redirect to system quality)
6. **"Can I try just one service instead of the bundle?"** (yes, but reframe why bundle is 4x the value)

### Phase 7: Close (13:00-15:00)
- Direct ask: "So — want to be one of the first 10 Founding Clients?"
- If YES: send Stripe link on call, get card, book onboarding kickoff for next day
- If MAYBE: book follow-up for 48h, send Founding Client offer summary + 2 case study links
- If NO: ask why (learning), thank them, keep door open

### Phase 8: Post-call follow-up (within 30 min of hang-up)
- Send WhatsApp/email recap
- Include: Founding Client offer summary, Stripe checkout link, testimonial link, calendar link for onboarding
- Set 24h and 72h follow-up reminders
