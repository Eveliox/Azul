# Deliver Website Building

### Scope and preview

1. With the owner, list the pages, languages, services, cities served, public phone number and primary action on the site. Record who approves copy and who owns the domain.
2. Request approved logos, job photos, business hours and contact details. Confirm which claims, offers and photos the owner is comfortable publishing.
3. Open **Website Building** in the client workspace. Use its checklist to track progress. Build and deploy the client’s site in its own hosting project.
4. Share the preview with the approver. On both a phone and desktop, walk through the path a customer would take from the home page to requesting service. Check each language, tap-to-call number, booking destination, privacy link and confirmation message.

### Route an enquiry

| Purchased service | What to verify on the preview |
|---|---|
| Website only | Submit an enquiry with your own contact details. Have the designated recipient read back the requested service and reply to the test inbox. |
| Website plus agent | Follow [agent setup](speed-to-lead.md). With the client in Testing, use your own listed test number. Confirm the enquiry appears once under the correct business with the right language and call permission. |

### Launch and handoff

1. Get the owner’s approval of the preview. Save the current website DNS records and previous working destination before changing the website records. Preserve the domain’s email records.
2. Publish to the agreed domain. Open the public HTTPS address and repeat the mobile contact path. Confirm the intended recipient sees the enquiry. For an agent bundle, coordinate activation with the agent launch checklist.
3. If the domain points to the wrong site or enquiries disappear, restore the previous working site or contact destination and verify it before directing traffic back.
4. Give the owner the public URL, hosting and domain account owners, enquiry destination, and the contact for edits or missed leads. Record the launch date and completed checks under **Website Building**.
