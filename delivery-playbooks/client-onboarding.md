# Set up a new client

**Azul does the setup. The owner confirms access.** Have the signed scope in front of you when choosing services.

### 1. Collect the kickoff details

| Ask the owner for | Where you use it |
|---|---|
| Business name, owner name, email, mobile, and who handles new enquiries | Workspace, invitations and handoff |
| Website Building, Speed-to-Lead Agent, or both, as agreed | Service selection |
| Domain, registrar or website contact, logo, approved photos, service list and service area | Website build |
| Name callers should hear, languages, time zone, hours, alert inbox, transfer number, booking calendar and who can share it | Agent setup |

Record the owner’s answers and open items in the private client record. Request delegated domain or calendar access from the account owner. Keep invitation links and provider credentials out of shared kickoff notes.

### 2. Create the workspace

1. Sign in with your named agency account at the [Azul dashboard](https://app.azulwebdev.com/admin).
2. Check **Select business** for an existing workspace. If one exists, open it and verify its plan before continuing.
3. For a new client, choose **Add business**. Enter the business name, a short ID such as `bayview-ac`, and the owner’s contact details. The short ID becomes part of the hosted form address if they use one.
4. Tick the purchased services and submit. Select the new business by name. In **Workspace access → Services in their plan**, verify the selection and use **Save plan** if it needs correction.

### 3. Give each person access

1. In **Workspace access → Individual team accounts**, enter the owner’s email and select **Create invitation**. Copy the private link and send it directly to that person. Azul must share the link manually.
2. Have the owner open it, use **Set or change your account password**, then sign out and sign back in with their email. Ask them to read back the business name and open their purchased service.
3. Repeat for the dispatcher or other approved staff using their individual addresses. Keep agency administrator access with Azul.

**If access looks wrong:** Check the selected business, saved plan and membership email. Remove a mistaken membership in **Individual team accounts**. For someone who already has an Azul account, use the same invitation flow. It creates a private sign-in link and adds membership for the selected business.

**Handoff record:** Save the business name and short ID, purchased services, people who verified sign-in, remaining inputs, person responsible and next check-in date. Continue to [Website Building](website-launch.md) or [Speed-to-Lead Agent](speed-to-lead.md).
