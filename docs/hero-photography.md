## Hand / phone generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: website hero photographic cutout for Azul, transparent background. Generate a photorealistic human hand naturally holding up a premium thin graphite smartphone, viewed from the front in a dramatic foreshortened three-quarter perspective, like a luxury fintech campaign. The wrist enters from bottom right, fingers wrap behind the device with the thumb touching the LEFT OUTER BEZEL only. Phone top leans to the left, phone bottom to the right, top is slightly farther away than bottom, tilted backward about 28 degrees. Entire phone visible, large occupying about 65 percent image height, wrist cropped at bottom. Real warm medium-brown skin with lifelike pores, creases, fingernails and physically correct grip. Soft white studio lighting with subtle cool periwinkle blue edge reflections. Thin dark metallic bezel with rounded corners. CRITICAL: phone display must be an entirely BLANK solid white screen without any text, icons, notch, logo or UI, fully unobstructed by fingers; we overlay a live HTML interface on that exact screen. Crisp realistic glass highlights only at outer edges. Transparent background with real alpha, no background color, no checkerboard, no ground plane, no drop shadow outside subject. Portrait 1024x1536 composition. Premium actual photography, not illustration or plastic CGI.

## Background generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: full bleed website hero background texture for Azul digital studio. Wide 1536x1024 image, no text, no phone, no objects, no logos. Photographic macro abstract flowing translucent silk and frosted optical glass, vast soft sweeping folds like fluid ribbons, a large curved swoosh sweeping diagonally from lower left toward upper right, luminous pearl white highlights and rich periwinkle blue shadows, atmospheric out of focus gradients with fine subtle analog photographic grain. Inspired by a premium fintech campaign's immersive liquid background but exclusively Azul colors: off-white #F5F7FA, airy ice blue, periwinkle #8FA3F0, occasional restrained Azul #3B5BDB. Upper third is mostly pale off-white with extremely subtle texture so dark navy headlines are perfectly legible. Lower center is soft pale blue to frame a photographic phone and hand. The right side has a wide sinuous pearlescent fold with softly shaded blue depth. High key overall, not dark, no neon, no green or yellow, no hard vector lines, no geometric blobs, no distinct horizon. Luxurious tangible material, soft cinematic lens blur, tactile fine grain.
