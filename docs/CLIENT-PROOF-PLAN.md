# Hello You and Aspire: credible before-and-after proof

No customer-growth, revenue, or conversion figures have been supplied or verified.
Do not publish estimated results attributed to either client without a documented source.

## Case-study layout

1. Before / after screenshots, labeled with capture dates and the same viewport.
2. The original business need, confirmed by the owner.
3. Work delivered, verified against the live site and delivery records.
4. Results panel, where each metric carries a source, time window and evidence label.
5. When historical data is unavailable: "Tracking started [verified date]. Results
   will be added as data becomes available." A missing baseline is not zero.

Use these evidence labels next to each number, not only in a footnote:

- **Measured:** supported by booking records, forms, CRM, phone logs or analytics.
- **Owner-reported estimate:** based on the owner's recollection or manual tally;
  include the date range, collection date, and the owner's approval of the quote.
- **Projected scenario:** hypothetical assumptions; keep outside client results.

## Metrics to establish

| Business | Website signals | Leads | Confirmed business outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hello You | Booking-link clicks, service-page visits | Enquiries / appointment requests | Confirmed appointments; attended appointments separately |
| Aspire | Quote-button clicks, service-page visits | Valid quote requests; answered enquiries | Estimates issued; accepted jobs separately |

These are proposed metrics, not claims that the services or tracking are deployed.
Count a click as a click, a submitted request as a lead, and a confirmed sale only
when it appears in the client's records. Do not add calls and form submissions
without deduplicating the same person. For Hello You, keep patient identities and
health-related details out of marketing analytics and public proof artifacts.

## Recover a usable baseline

Ask each owner for aggregate weekly/monthly counts from existing records: enquiry
emails, booking-system totals, quote records and completed jobs. Compare equal-length
periods with comparable opening days. Record changes in advertising spend, seasonal
demand, staffing and business hours alongside any before/after comparison.

A reconstructed baseline is labeled "Reconstructed from [source]", and any missing
coverage is stated. A rise after the redesign is a comparison; it does not establish
that Azul caused the entire increase. For attributed results, retain campaign/source
information and confirm outcomes against client records.

## Optional scenario calculator

Keep this separate from both case studies. Use editable assumptions and no preset
client claims:

additional enquiries × booking/close rate = potential additional bookings/jobs

Example only: 10 additional enquiries × 20% booking rate = 2 potential bookings.
Display: "Illustrative scenario, not measured client results or a forecast."
Do not calculate a growth percentage when the baseline is unknown or zero.

## First 30 days of measurement

- Confirm the owner and data source for each metric.
- Validate tracking with test submissions, excluding test traffic from results.
- Track successful enquiries separately from booking/quote link clicks.
- Review weekly aggregates and reconcile leads with confirmed outcomes.
- Get owner approval before publishing screenshots, quotations or metrics.
