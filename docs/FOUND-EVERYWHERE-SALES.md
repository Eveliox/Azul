**Q: What are "AI citations" and why should I care?**
When someone asks ChatGPT "who's the best roofer in Miami," ChatGPT names specific companies. Those names come from sources like Reddit, local news, and authority lists — not from Google rankings. If you're not in those sources, you're not getting cited. The customers using AI to search will never hear your name.

**Q: Do you really do everything in Spanish?**
Yes. Every post, every Reddit answer, every YouTube caption, every monthly report — fully bilingual. Miami customers switch between English and Spanish mid-sentence. We built the whole system around that.

**Q: What if I already have an SEO company?**
Keep them. Found Everywhere covers the AI side plus Google Business Profile — most SEO agencies don't touch either. If you want to consolidate later, we're happy to take over.

**Q: Can you guarantee I'll rank #1 on Google?**
No, and anyone who guarantees that is lying. We guarantee the opposite side: cited by at least one AI engine on your agreed target queries within 90 days, or month 3 is refunded.

**Q: What if I want to cancel?**
30 days notice, no fees, no questions. You keep your Google Business Profile, your Reddit account, your YouTube channel, your schema markup. We never hold your accounts hostage.

## 4. Cold-call script

Keep it under 90 seconds to the pitch. The ChatGPT demo is the magic — do not skip it.

### Opening (10 seconds)

> *"Hey [first name], Evelio from Azul in Miami. Super quick — I help [roofing / HVAC / plumbing] companies in [their city] show up when customers search online. Got 60 seconds for me to show you something weird about your business?"*

If "I'm busy": *"Totally fair. One question before I go — have you ever asked ChatGPT who the best [their trade] in [city] is? Because that's what I was going to show you. When would be a better time? I'll call you back."*

### En español

> *"Hola [nombre], soy Evelio de Azul en Miami. Rapidito — ayudo a compañías de [techos / A/C / plomería] en [ciudad] a aparecer cuando los clientes buscan en línea. ¿Tiene 60 segundos para mostrarle algo raro sobre su negocio?"*

### The hook (10 seconds)

> *"Open ChatGPT or Google on your phone right now. Type: 'best [their service] in [their city].' I'll wait."*

Pause. Let them actually do it. If they resist, pivot: *"If you can't do it right now, no problem — can I text you a 30-second video of what shows up? Three names, your company isn't one of them."*

### The reveal (20 seconds)

Whatever name comes up:

> *"See those three or four companies? Those are the ones ChatGPT, Siri, and Google's AI are telling customers to call. By next year, that's where 60% of local search is going — not Google results, AI answers. The companies that get named get the calls. Your company isn't getting named — not because you're worse, but because you're not in the sources AI pulls from. Reddit, local news, YouTube, local authority lists. That's what I fix."*

### The pitch (20 seconds)

> *"It's one monthly service — $599, no contract. Here's what you get every month: four posts on your Google profile, one placement on a local authority site, two Reddit mentions, one short YouTube video from your real jobs, and a monthly report where I literally ask ChatGPT about you so you can see it working. Everything in English and Spanish. If you're not getting cited by AI in 90 days, I refund month three. Yes or no — want me to run a free audit this week?"*

### En español

> *"Es un servicio mensual — $599, sin contrato. Esto es lo que recibe cada mes: cuatro publicaciones en su perfil de Google, un enlace en un sitio de autoridad local, dos menciones en Reddit, un video corto de YouTube de sus trabajos reales, y un reporte mensual donde yo literalmente le pregunto a ChatGPT sobre su negocio para que vea los resultados. Todo en inglés y español. Si no sale citado por la IA en 90 días, le devuelvo el mes tres. Sí o no — ¿quiere que le haga una auditoría gratis esta semana?"*

## 5. The free audit (the trojan horse)

The "free visibility audit" is the top-of-funnel offer. It takes you ~45 minutes per prospect and converts well because it shows them the gap in black and white.

**Deliverable: 2-minute Loom video + 1-page PDF, bilingual if they prefer Spanish.**

**What's in the audit:**
1. **ChatGPT query:** "Best [their service] in [their city]" — screenshot the result. Are they named? Who is?
2. **Perplexity query:** same. Screenshot sources Perplexity cites.
3. **Google AI Overviews:** same query in Google. Screenshot the AI Overview panel.
4. **Google Business Profile:** category count, service area, review count, last post date.
5. **LocalFalcon mini-scan:** 1 keyword, grid view.
6. **One-sentence verdict:** *"You're missing from [X of 3] AI engines and [Y] of your Google Business Profile basics are incomplete. Here's what we'd fix in the first 30 days."*

Send via Loom + PDF with a book-a-call CTA. If they don't book within 48 hours, follow up once with: *"Did the audit video make sense? Happy to walk you through it live for 15 min."*

## 6. Where to run this

**Highest-converting outbound channels for this offer:**
1. **In-person visits to job sites and showrooms** — Miami home-service owners are hands-on; cold calls get screened, in-person doesn't.
2. **WhatsApp cold outreach** — bilingual, feels personal, Miami norm.
3. **Instagram DM to companies with weak social presence** — if their last IG post was 6 months ago, they're a perfect fit.
4. **BNI / Miami chamber of commerce events** — the demo move works even better live.
5. **Referrals from existing Review Booster clients** — "I helped X get 40 reviews, now I'm helping them show up in ChatGPT. Know anyone else who should see this?"

Skip paid ads at this stage. The demo move needs a live human to land. Build it on 1:1 outbound until you have 5-10 case studies, then automate.
